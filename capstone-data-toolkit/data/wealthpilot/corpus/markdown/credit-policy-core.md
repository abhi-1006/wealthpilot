# Ashva Capital Credit Policy Manual: Core Underwriting Standards

**CREDIT POLICY MANUAL**
**Ashva Capital**
**SME Term Loans**

**Section 1: General Underwriting Standards**

1.1 **Debt-Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)**: At least 1.25x DSCR is required for all SME term loan applications.

1.2 **Operating History**: A minimum of 24 months of operating history is required for all SME term loan applicants.

**Section 2: Leverage Limitations**

2.1 **Maximum Leverage**: The borrower's total debt-to-equity (TDE) ratio must not exceed 3:1.

**Section 3: Liquidity Requirements**

3.1 **Current-Ratio Floor**: The borrower's current ratio must be at least 1.5:1.

**Section 4: Credit Bureau Scorebands**

4.1 **Bureau Scorebands**:

- **Band A (720+)**: Loans up to 2,500,000 rupees may be approved by a Loan Officer.
- **Band B (700-719)**: Loans up to 1,500,000 rupees may be approved by a Loan Officer.
- **Band C (680-699)**: Loans up to 1,000,000 rupees may be approved by a Loan Officer.
- **Band D (660-679)**: Loans up to 750,000 rupees may be approved by a Loan Officer.

**Section 5: Approval Authorities and Loan Tiers**

5.1 **Loan Tiers and Approval Authorities**:

- **Tier 1: Up to 500,000 rupees**: May be approved by a Loan Officer without requiring a Loan Committee approval.
- **Tier 2: 500,000 - 1,000,000 rupees**: Requires a Loan Committee approval.
- **Tier 3: 1,000,000 - 2,500,000 rupees**: Requires a Chief Credit Officer approval.
- **Tier 4: 2,500,000 - 5,000,000 rupees**: Requires a Board approval.
- **Tier 5: Above 5,000,000 rupees**: Requires a Board approval with a human signatory of the Board of Directors.

5.2 **No loan above 5,000,000 rupees may be approved without a human signatory from the Board of Directors.**

**Section 6: Exceptions**

6.1 **Exception for Specialized Industries**: In the case of applicants from specialized industries such as technology or pharmaceuticals with a history of consistent profitability and growth, the Board may approve loans above 5,000,000 rupees without a human signatory if such a loan does not exceed 7,500,000 rupees and has the prior approval of the Chief Credit Officer.

6.2 **Exception for Refinancing Existing Loans**: For the refinancing of existing loans with Ashva Capital, the Loan Officer may review and approve loans up to 5,000,000 rupees without a human signatory if the borrower's credit profile has not significantly deteriorated from the time of the original loan approval.
